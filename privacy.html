Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Compliant with Discord Developer Policies

1. Overview

RubberDuck ("the Bot", "we", "our") is designed to provide utility, gamification, and moderation support for Discord servers. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data to provide bot functionality:

  • Identifiers: Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs.
  • Progression & Economy: Virtual XP counters, level statistics, virtual coin balances, cosmetic inventory items, and unlocked achievement flags.
  • User-Configured Data: Persistent reminders, event titles and scheduled dates, and TODO list entries.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

We respect user privacy and adhere to zero-logging principles:

  • We never store message content or message histories in persistent databases.
  • Incoming messages are parsed exclusively in volatile memory for activity XP calculations and keyword triggers, then immediately discarded.
  • We do not collect IP addresses, real names, payment information, or external credentials.

4. Data Storage & Security

All persistent bot data is stored in isolated, cloud-hosted relational databases with strict TLS encryption in transit and access controls. Database access is restricted strictly to the bot instance.

5. Data Erasure & User Rights

In accordance with Discord Developer Policies and GDPR principles, any user or server administrator may request complete deletion of their stored statistics, reminders, and inventory data by contacting the bot developers. Removing the bot from a server halts data synchronization immediately.
